# Jackie Ng — Personal Brand Identity

## Positioning Statement
"I show the algorithms running inside molecules — then I build tools around what I find."

For drug discovery teams who need to understand protein-ligand binding dynamics, Jackie provides computational structural biology tools that turn static crystal structures into dynamic simulation landscapes, unlike traditional bioinformatics workflows that require weeks of specialist setup, because his platform bridges cryo-EM validated data with one-click molecular dynamics.

## Brand Archetype: Creator × Sage
Someone who sees deep patterns others miss AND builds from that understanding. Not the Hero (competition warrior). Not the Explorer (curious wanderer). The person who says: "I see something in this structure that you don't — and I built a tool so you can see it too."

## Core Differentiation
Jackie doesn't explain biology. He shows the **computation running inside biological systems.** That's what separates him from every other structural biology DPhil student.

- Proteins are not "targets" — they're **constraint satisfaction problems**
- Binding is not "interaction" — it's **energy landscape exploration**
- Drug discovery is not "finding cures" — it's **navigating search space**

Could any other DPhil student have this page? If yes → not differentiated.

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- Confident and precise, not boastful
- Fascinated by the work, not performing fascination
- Builder mindset: see a problem → build the tool
- First person. Short sentences. Opinions.

### Allowed Framing
- Engineering/architecture metaphors for molecules
- Computation/algorithm metaphors for biology
- Mathematics/physics framing for structures and dynamics

### Forbidden
- Generic bio (immune army, cells attacking, drug as weapon)
- Medical warfare metaphors
- Age-bragging ("before turning 26")
- Brand handle ("The Invisible Architect") on personal profile

## Visual Identity
- Dark (#0a0a0a) + gold (#9d7e5a) + teal (#2a9d8f)
- Real molecular renders as visual language (never stock, never AI-generated)
- Serif (Cormorant Garamond) for authority, sans (Inter) for precision
- Editorial layout: asymmetric, grid-breaking, full-bleed molecular breaks
- Photography: on stage, in real rooms — not headshots
