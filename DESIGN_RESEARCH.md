## Key Findings: What Exceptional Sites DO

### 1. **Clarity Over Cleverness**

**Stripe:**
> "Stripe is a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth and prosperity."

**Linear:**
> "Software development is at an inflection point. Artificial intelligence is fundamentally reshaping how products are built."

**Pattern:** ONE clear sentence. No jargon. No positioning statements. Just what they do.

### 2. **Content First, Always**

**Anthropic docs:**
- Code examples immediately visible (9 languages)
- "Get API key" button in hero
- Platform → Journey → Models (clear hierarchy)

**Sequoia:**
- List of recent articles/podcasts immediately visible
- "We help the daring build legendary companies" (one line, then content)

**Ben Kuhn:**
- "Hi, I'm Ben" (one paragraph)
- Essays immediately visible with summaries
- No hero section, just content

**Pattern:** Show work immediately. Don't make people hunt for substance.

### 3. **One Paragraph Maximum**

**Andy Matuschak:**
> "I'm an applied researcher, focused on creating user interfaces that expand what people can think and do."

**Patrick Collison:**
- Just his name + list of topics (Advice, Bookshelf, Culture, etc.)
- ZERO bio text

**Pattern:** If you need more than one paragraph to introduce yourself, you're overthinking it.

### 4. **No Marketing Language**

❌ **Bad:**
- "Leading expert in..."
- "Revolutionary platform..."
- "World-class..."
- "Passionate about..."

✅ **Good:**
- "I work on X"
- "We build Y"
- "This does Z"

**Pattern:** Describe what you do, not how amazing you are.

### 5. **Typography as Hierarchy (Not Decoration)**

**Common patterns:**
- **Hero:** 1-2 sentences, large (48-72px)
- **Section headers:** Clear, bold, 24-32px
- **Body:** Readable, 16-18px, generous line-height (1.5-1.6)
- **Links:** Underlined or clearly distinct
- **Whitespace:** 50-70% of the page

**Pattern:** Size = importance. No color tricks needed.

### 6. **Fast to Load, Easy to Scan**

**All sites:**
- No animations (or minimal, purposeful ones)
- No hero videos
- No particle effects
- Static content loads instantly
- Clear sections with headings

**Pattern:** Every millisecond of load time costs attention.

---

## What Exceptional Sites DON'T Do

❌ **Hero sections with taglines** (unless it's ONE clear sentence)  
❌ **About storytelling** ("My journey began when...")  
❌ **Resume-style lists** (10+ achievements in bullet points)  
❌ **Particle animations** (gimmicky)  
❌ **Dark mode toggles** (pick one, commit)  
❌ **Complex layouts** (multi-column grids, tabs, accordions)  
❌ **Multiple CTAs** ("Download," "Learn More," "Contact," "Subscribe")  
❌ **Empty marketing copy** ("innovative," "cutting-edge," "passionate")

## Design Principles Extracted

### **Principle 1: Immediate Clarity**
Visitor should understand WHO you are and WHAT you do in <5 seconds.

### **Principle 2: Work > Words**
Show actual work (papers, projects, writing, code) instead of describing yourself.

### **Principle 3: One Thing at a Time**
Each section has ONE clear purpose. No multi-tasking content.

### **Principle 4: Hierarchy Through Size**
- Most important = biggest
- Less important = smaller
- Use size/weight, not color/animation

### **Principle 5: Restrained Palette**
- 2-3 colors maximum
- Black/white base + one accent
- No gradients, no shadows, no effects

### **Principle 6: Typography Confidence**
- Big when it matters (hero: 48-72px)
- Readable for body (16-18px)
- Generous whitespace (1.5-1.6 line-height)
- Serif for authority, Sans for precision

### **Principle 7: Speed**
- <1s load time
- No dependencies (or minimal)
- Static > Dynamic
- Plain HTML/CSS > JavaScript frameworks

## Application to Jackie's Site

### **What to Keep:**
- Simple structure (HTML/CSS only)
- Fast load time
- Clear typography

### **What to Change:**

1. **Replace "personal brand page" with index of work**
   - Current: "I predict which systems will work..."
   - Better: "Jackie Ng. I work on drug delivery. Here's what I've built:"

2. **Show actual work, not explanations**
   - Current: Size comparison diagram (explaining the platform)
   - Better: Link to platform demo, papers, talks

3. **One paragraph introduction max**
   - Current: Hero + facts + recognition grid
   - Better: Name + one sentence + list of work

4. **Remove marketing framing**
   - No "personal brand"
   - No "positioning statements"
   - Just work

## Template Structure (Based on Research)

```
[Name]
[One sentence: what you do]

[Section: Papers/Publications]
- Link 1
- Link 2

[Section: Projects/Tools]
- Link 1
- Link 2

[Section: Writing]
- Link 1
- Link 2

[Section: Talks]
- Link 1
- Link 2

[Email]
```

**That's it.** No hero. No diagram. No recognition grid. Just an index to actual work.
