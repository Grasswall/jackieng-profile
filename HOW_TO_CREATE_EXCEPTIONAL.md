# How to Create an Exceptional Personal Brand Page
**Planning Framework for Jackie Ng's Brand Site**  
**Date:** 2026-10-26

---

## The Core Question

**What makes a personal brand page "exceptional"?**

Not "good" or "professional" — **exceptional**. The kind that:
- Gets you remembered 6 months later
- Changes how people perceive your work
- Opens doors you didn't know existed
- Makes recruiters/VCs/collaborators reach out unprompted

---

## The Research Plan

### **Phase 1: Define "Exceptional" for YOUR Context**

Before copying anyone else's site, answer:

#### **1A. Who needs to be impressed?**
Not "everyone" — be specific:
- [ ] Pharma R&D executives (hiring for platform roles)
- [ ] VCs evaluating biotech investments
- [ ] Academic collaborators (structural biology/drug delivery)
- [ ] Media/conference organizers (speaking opportunities)
- [ ] Regulatory/partnership targets (AtomBios business development)

**Action:** Rank these 1-5. Top 2 are your design constraints.

#### **1B. What action defines success?**
What happens after someone visits your site that proves it worked?

Possible metrics:
- [ ] Inbound interview requests
- [ ] VC meeting requests
- [ ] Collaboration proposals
- [ ] Speaking invitations
- [ ] Media interview requests
- [ ] LinkedIn connection requests from target companies

**Action:** Pick ONE primary success metric. Everything else is secondary.

#### **1C. What's your unfair advantage?**
What can you show that competitors can't?

For Jackie, candidates:
- [ ] Cryo-EM structures (visual proof of technical skill)
- [ ] AtomBios platform data (BBB crossing, tumor penetration)
- [ ] Elite network access (Lindau, Shaw Prize, RTHK media)
- [ ] Cross-domain depth (structural biology → engineering → business)
- [ ] Speed of execution (HK$7M funding, multiple structures, media presence)

**Action:** Which 2-3 unfair advantages should be impossible to miss?

### **Phase 2: Study the Right Comparables**

Don't study random "best portfolios" — study people who ACTUALLY got the outcome you want.

#### **2A. Find 10 people who have what you want**
Examples:
- Scientists who moved from academia → pharma R&D leadership
- PhD students who raised VC funding during their degree
- Structural biologists who became startup founders
- Biotech founders who got acquired/IPO'd
- Researchers who became media/thought leaders

**Action:** 
```
For each person:
- [ ] Find their personal website (if it exists)
- [ ] Screenshot the homepage
- [ ] Note: What's above the fold?
- [ ] Note: What's their first sentence?
- [ ] Note: What proof do they lead with?
- [ ] Note: What's the primary CTA?
```

#### **2B. Reverse-engineer their positioning**
For each comparable, ask:
- What do they want you to believe about them?
- What evidence supports that belief?
- What's NOT on their site (what they're hiding/de-emphasizing)?
- Would their site work for YOUR goals?

**Action:** Create a comparison table:
```
| Person | Positioning | Evidence Shown | Missing Elements | Steal This |
|--------|-------------|----------------|------------------|------------|
| [Name] | "Platform expert" | 3 patents, 2 partnerships | No publications | CTA placement |
```

#### **2C. Study outside your field**
Look at exceptional personal brands in OTHER domains who face similar challenges:

- **Designer selling to enterprise:** How do they prove ROI?
- **Consultant positioning for C-suite:** How do they show strategic thinking?
- **Academic turned founder:** How do they balance credibility + entrepreneurship?
- **Technical expert for non-technical audience:** How do they simplify without dumbing down?

**Action:** Find 3 cross-domain examples and note what translates.

### **Phase 4: Define Your Positioning**

Now that you know your audience, comparables, and assets — what's your ONE thing?

#### **4A. The Positioning Statement**

Fill in the blanks:
```
I help [TARGET AUDIENCE] 
[ACHIEVE GOAL / SOLVE PROBLEM]
by [UNIQUE APPROACH / UNFAIR ADVANTAGE]
unlike [ALTERNATIVES / COMPETITORS]
proven by [CONCRETE EVIDENCE].
```

Example for Jackie:
```
I help pharma R&D teams
overcome the delivery barrier
by designing nanoparticles from first principles using structural biology
unlike empirical screening or AI-only approaches
proven by 8× BBB transcytosis and HK$7M in validated funding.
```

**Action:** Write 5 versions. Pick the one that feels true AND compelling.

#### **4B. The Proof Hierarchy**

What evidence supports your positioning, ranked by strength?

```
TIER 1 (Undeniable):
- Published papers
- Deposited structures
- Validated funding
- Partnerships with established companies

TIER 2 (Strong):
- Competition wins/placements
- Elite network access
- Media coverage
- Conference invitations

TIER 3 (Signals):
- Work in progress
- Preprints
- Testimonials
- Follower count
```

**Action:** List your top 5 proofs. These must be above the fold.

#### **4C. The Narrative Arc**

Every exceptional site tells a story. What's yours?

Structure options:

**A. Problem → Solution → Proof**
```
The delivery barrier kills 90% of drugs.
I engineer nanoparticles that cross it.
Here's the data.
```

**B. Expertise → Application → Impact**
```
I see structures at atomic resolution.
I use that to design delivery systems.
This changes what's possible in medicine.
```

**C. Journey → Insight → Platform**
```
I spent years solving protein structures.
I realized the same principles apply to delivery.
So I built a platform.
```

**Action:** Pick one narrative structure. Everything flows from this.

### **Phase 5: Design the Experience**

Now you know WHAT to say. How do you say it?

#### **5A. The First 3 Seconds**

When someone lands on your site, what do they see?

**Test:** Can a stranger answer these in 3 seconds?
- [ ] Who is this person?
- [ ] What do they do?
- [ ] Why should I care?
- [ ] What can I do here?

**Bad example (current site):**
```
Jackie Ng
I work on drug delivery. Specifically, designing nanoparticles that cross biological barriers — the blood-brain barrier, the blood-tumor barrier, and the endosomal membrane.
```
→ Clear but not compelling. No urgency. No visual hook.

**Better example:**
```
[Visual: Molecular structure rotating]

Jackie Ng
I design the 20nm nanoparticles that cross the blood-brain barrier 8× better than current methods.

Structural biologist → Drug delivery engineer → AtomBios founder

[View Platform] [Research] [Contact]
```
→ Visual + specific claim + proof of versatility + clear actions.

**Action:** Write 3 versions of your 3-second pitch. Test on someone unfamiliar.

#### **5B. The Scroll Journey**

After the first 3 seconds, what's the path?

Map the emotional journey:
```
CURIOSITY → CREDIBILITY → DEPTH → CONNECTION → ACTION

Section 1: Hook (Now / Current Work)
  Emotion: Curiosity
  Goal: "This person is active and interesting"
  
Section 2: Proof (Platform / Key Work)
  Emotion: Credibility
  Goal: "This person has real results"
  
Section 3: Depth (Publications / Structures)
  Emotion: Respect
  Goal: "This person is technically serious"
  
Section 4: Context (Recognition / Network)
  Emotion: Social proof
  Goal: "Others value this person"
  
Section 5: Personality (Writing / Talks)
  Emotion: Connection
  Goal: "I want to work with this person"
  
Section 6: Action (Contact / CTA)
  Emotion: Readiness
  Goal: "I know what to do next"
```

**Action:** Map your sections to emotional beats. Does the flow make sense?

#### **5C. The Visual Identity**

What should it FEEL like to be on your site?

Mood options:

**Scientific Precision:**
- Clean, structured, data-driven
- Black/white + one accent
- Monospace + serif
- Think: Nature journal

**Builder Confidence:**
- Bold, modern, forward-looking
- Dark backgrounds + bright highlights
- Sans-serif + geometric shapes
- Think: Stripe, Linear

**Molecular Beauty:**
- Elegant, surprising, atmospheric
- Gradients + depth + motion
- Display font + technical details
- Think: High-end design agency meets science

**Action:** Choose one mood. Collect 5 visual references that match.

**Action:** Build 3 visual directions. Pick based on feedback.

### **Phase 7: Technical Execution**

Now you're ready to build:

#### **7A. Tech Stack Decision**

Options:

**A. Static HTML/CSS (current approach)**
- ✅ Full control, fast, no dependencies
- ✅ Easy to deploy (GitHub Pages)
- ❌ Every change requires code edits
- ❌ No CMS for future content

**B. Static Site Generator (Hugo, Jekyll, Eleventy)**
- ✅ Markdown for content
- ✅ Templates for consistency
- ✅ Fast builds, static output
- ❌ Learning curve for setup

**C. Modern Builder (Webflow, Framer, Super)**
- ✅ Visual editing, fast iteration
- ✅ Built-in CMS
- ✅ Professional templates
- ❌ Monthly cost
- ❌ Vendor lock-in

**D. Framework (Next.js, Astro, Svelte)**
- ✅ Component architecture
- ✅ Dynamic features (search, filters)
- ✅ Optimizations built-in
- ❌ Overkill for single-page portfolio
- ❌ Build complexity

**Action:** Based on maintenance tolerance, pick one.

#### **7B. Performance Targets**

Set measurable goals:

```
LIGHTHOUSE SCORES:
- [ ] Performance: >90
- [ ] Accessibility: >95
- [ ] Best Practices: >95
- [ ] SEO: >90

CORE WEB VITALS:
- [ ] LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): <2.5s
- [ ] FID (First Input Delay): <100ms
- [ ] CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): <0.1

FILE SIZES:
- [ ] Total HTML+CSS: <50KB
- [ ] Hero image: <200KB
- [ ] Other images: <100KB each
- [ ] Total page weight: <1MB

MOBILE:
- [ ] Readable on 320px width
- [ ] Touch targets >44px
- [ ] No horizontal scroll
- [ ] Fast on 3G connection
```

**Action:** Test every deploy against these benchmarks.

#### **7C. SEO Foundation**

Make sure you're discoverable:

```
TECHNICAL SEO:
- [ ] Title tag: "Jackie Ng — Drug Delivery Researcher & AtomBios Founder"
- [ ] Meta description: Your positioning statement (155 chars)
- [ ] Open Graph tags (LinkedIn/Twitter preview)
- [ ] Canonical URL
- [ ] Sitemap.xml
- [ ] robots.txt (allow all)

```

**Action:** Validate with Google Rich Results Test.

### **Phase 8: Launch & Iterate**

Don't wait for perfect. Launch at 80% and improve based on data.

#### **8A. Soft Launch**
Before announcing publicly:
```
WEEK 1: Friends & Family
- Share with 5 people
- Ask: "What's unclear? What's missing?"
- Fix obvious issues

WEEK 2: Target Audience Sample
- Share with 3 people from each audience (pharma, VC, academic)
- Ask: "Would you reach out? Why/why not?"
- Fix positioning issues

WEEK 3: Analytics Setup
- [ ] Google Analytics or Plausible
- [ ] Track: page views, scroll depth, clicks on CTAs
- [ ] Set up goals for primary action (CV download, contact email, etc.)
```

**Action:** Don't announce until soft launch feedback is incorporated.

#### **8B. Public Launch**
Once it's ready:
```
ANNOUNCE:
- [ ] LinkedIn post (personal + AtomBios page)
- [ ] Twitter/X thread
- [ ] Email to key contacts
- [ ] Update email signatures
- [ ] Update all bios (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.)

MONITOR:
- [ ] Check analytics daily for first week
- [ ] Note: which sections get scrolled to?
- [ ] Note: which CTAs get clicked?
- [ ] Note: any 404s or broken links?
```

**Action:** Set reminder to check analytics 1 week, 1 month, 3 months post-launch.

#### **8C. Continuous Improvement**

The site is never "done":

```
MONTHLY:
- [ ] Update "Now" section with current work
- [ ] Add new publications/talks/recognition
- [ ] Check for broken links
- [ ] Review analytics (what's working?)

QUARTERLY:
- [ ] Refresh homepage hero (new project/achievement)
- [ ] Add new case study or featured work
- [ ] Update CV
- [ ] Review positioning (still accurate?)

YEARLY:
- [ ] Full content audit (what's outdated?)
- [ ] Design refresh (still looks modern?)
- [ ] Tech stack review (still serving needs?)
- [ ] Backup all content (in case site goes down)
```

**Action:** Add reminders to calendar NOW.

## The Exceptional Standard: Checklist

Before calling your site "exceptional," it must pass this test:

### **Instant Impact (First 3 Seconds)**
- [ ] I know who you are
- [ ] I know what you do
- [ ] I see visual proof (image/structure/data)
- [ ] I know what to do next

### **Credibility (First 30 Seconds)**
- [ ] I see concrete evidence (publications, data, funding)
- [ ] I see social proof (recognition, network, media)
- [ ] I see freshness signals (dates, recent work)
- [ ] I trust this person is real and active

### **Depth (First 3 Minutes)**
- [ ] I understand the technical depth
- [ ] I see the breadth of work
- [ ] I get a sense of personality
- [ ] I have enough to make a decision (hire, fund, collaborate)

### **Memorability (After Leaving)**
- [ ] I remember ONE thing about this person
- [ ] I would return to this site later
- [ ] I would share this site with others
- [ ] I would reach out if I had a relevant opportunity

### **Action (Conversion)**
- [ ] I know how to contact this person
- [ ] I know what kind of opportunities they want
- [ ] The CTA is obvious and friction-free
- [ ] I feel invited to reach out (not intimidated)
