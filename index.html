The Journey

I started in a biochemistry lab at HKUST, staring at protein structures on a screen, wondering: What if we could predict how drugs bind before spending years in the lab?

That question led me through cryo-EM research, computational drug design, and eventually to founding AtomBios — where we're building simulation tools that let scientists explore molecular mechanisms in real time.

Along the way, I've been fortunate to present at Nobel Laureate meetings, Shaw Prize roundtables, and symposiums across Asia. But the real work is still ahead: making computational biology fast, accessible, and beautiful enough that every researcher can use it.

What I'm Building

AtomBios
