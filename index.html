From the lab bench to the world stage

I started as a biochemistry student at HKUST. Then I saw my first cryo-EM structure — a protein frozen mid-action, every atom in place — and realized molecular mechanisms aren't abstract. They're blueprints you can see, simulate, and build tools around.

That insight led me from a First Class Honours degree to founding AtomBios, winning a Techathon+ Gold Medal, and being selected by the Nobel Foundation — all before turning 26.
