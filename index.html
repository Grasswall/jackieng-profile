AtomBios

Simulation at Scale —
built for pharma speed

AtomBios is a computational biology platform designed for drug developers who need answers fast — not in the next grant cycle.

  • Visualize protein–ligand interactions in real time, not after weeks of simulation setup
  • Test binding hypotheses before committing synthesis budget — reduce costly late-stage failures
  • One-click molecular dynamics: your team runs experiments, not IT tickets
  • Built on cryo-EM validated structural data — the science holds up under scrutiny
