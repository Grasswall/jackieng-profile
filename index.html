About

The work.

I'm a computational biologist working at the intersection of structural biology and drug discovery. My research uses cryo-EM validated structures to understand protein–ligand interactions — the atomic-level detail that determines whether a drug works or doesn't.

I founded AtomBios to make molecular simulation accessible to drug teams who need answers in hours, not months. The platform is incubated at Hong Kong Science and Technology Park and lets researchers test binding hypotheses without requiring dedicated computational infrastructure.

Before that, I graduated First Class from HKUST and published on SARS-CoV-2 structural mechanisms in the Journal of Biological Chemistry. My DPhil at PolyU continues that thread — structures, mechanisms, molecules.

I'm based in Hong Kong and occasionally speak at forums across the region on computational approaches to drug discovery.
