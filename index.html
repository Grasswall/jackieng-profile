AtomBios

I built the tool I wished existed

Every drug team I talked to had the same problem: they could see a protein structure, but couldn't test what happens when a molecule docks. AtomBios makes that instant. One-click molecular dynamics. 1,000 binding modes tested before a single compound is synthesized. Incubated at Hong Kong Science and Technology Park.

  • One-click molecular dynamics simulation
  • 1,000 binding modes tested per target
  • Structure-to-insight in minutes, not weeks
  • Built on validated cryo-EM structural data
  • Designed for early-stage drug discovery teams
