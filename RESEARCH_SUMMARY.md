# Design Research Summary: What Actually Works

## Key Finding
**The best personal sites are NOT "personal brand pages" — they're indexes to actual work.**

---

## What Exceptional Sites DO

### 1. **Clarity Over Cleverness**
- ONE clear sentence about what you do
- No jargon, no positioning statements
- Example (Stripe): "Stripe is a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth."

### 2. **Content First, Always**
- Show work immediately (papers, projects, code)
- Don't make people hunt for substance
- Example (Ben Kuhn): "Hi, I'm Ben" + immediate list of essays

### 3. **One Paragraph Maximum**
- If you need more than one paragraph, you're overthinking it
- Example (Patrick Collison): Just his name + list of topics (zero bio text)

### 4. **No Marketing Language**
- ❌ "Leading expert," "Revolutionary platform," "Passionate about"
- ✅ "I work on X," "We build Y," "This does Z"

### 5. **Typography as Hierarchy**
- Hero: 1-2 sentences, 48-72px
- Section headers: 24-32px
- Body: 16-18px, 1.5-1.6 line-height
- 50-70% whitespace

---

## What Exceptional Sites DON'T Do

❌ Hero sections with taglines  
❌ About storytelling ("My journey began...")  
❌ Resume-style achievement lists  
❌ Particle animations (gimmicky)  
❌ Dark mode toggles (pick one)  
❌ Complex layouts  
❌ Multiple CTAs  
❌ Empty marketing copy ("innovative," "cutting-edge")

**That's it.** No hero. No diagrams. No recognition grid. Just an index to actual work.

## Design Principles

1. **Immediate Clarity** — Understand WHO/WHAT in <5 seconds
2. **Work > Words** — Show actual work, not descriptions
3. **One Thing at a Time** — Each section has ONE purpose
4. **Hierarchy Through Size** — Most important = biggest
5. **Restrained Palette** — 2-3 colors max (black/white + accent)
6. **Typography Confidence** — Big when it matters (48-72px hero)
7. **Speed** — <1s load, static > dynamic
