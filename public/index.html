Work

Platform

20nm Programmable Nanoliposome Delivery System

  • 4-5× smaller than conventional LNPs (20nm vs 80-100nm)
  • 8× blood-brain barrier transcytosis efficiency
  • 3.2-fold brain accumulation (p<0.01)
  • Enhanced tumor penetration (size-dependent EPR effect)
  • Reduced immunogenicity (below immune surveillance thresholds)

Design approach: AI models predicted optimal size, surface chemistry, and lipid composition for BBB crossing. Structural biology insights into endosomal sorting guided targeting strategy. Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulated nanoparticle behavior at biological interfaces.

Characterization: DLS, zeta potential, encapsulation efficiency, stability (thermal, pH, serum), in vivo PK/PD (brain accumulation, biodistribution).

Validation: HK$7.3M funded · HKSTP Incu-Bio (national selection, 1/1000 acceptance) · Early pharma partnerships
